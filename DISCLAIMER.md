### Important Notices

1. **Consult a Professional.** Always seek the advice of a board-certified dermatologist or qualified healthcare provider before starting any hair loss treatment.

2. **Evidence, Not Prescription.** The protocols generated by HairStack are based on published clinical studies and are intended for educational purposes. They do not constitute a medical prescription.

3. **Individual Variation.** Hair loss treatment outcomes vary significantly between individuals based on genetics, health conditions, concurrent medications, and other factors.

4. **Side Effects.** All treatments, including over-the-counter products, carry potential side effects. Some treatments discussed in HairStack (such as finasteride) have documented risks that must be discussed with a physician.

5. **No Liability.** The creators and contributors of HairStack are not medical professionals and accept no liability for treatment outcomes. Use this information at your own risk.

6. **Citations.** Every treatment recommendation in HairStack links to published clinical studies (PubMed, NIH, FDA). We encourage you to read the original research and discuss it with your doctor.
